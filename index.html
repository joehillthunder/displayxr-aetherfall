Gold from mines, lumber from forests, and aether from glowing geysers. Build an extractor on a geyser to fuel siege engines, flyers and spellcasters.
An original game inspired by classic fantasy real-time strategy. Glasses-free 3D with DisplayXR on a 3D display, 2D everywhere else.

How to play

Mine gold, cut lumber and draw aether, build up your town, train an army and destroy every enemy building. Your hero gains levels from kills and unlocks a new ability at levels 1, 2 and 3.

SelectLeft-click, drag a box, Shift+click to add, double-click or Ctrl+click for all of a type on screen
CommandRight-click: move, attack, gather (mine, tree, extractor), resume a building, set a rally point
Attack-move, Move, Stop, HoldA M S H
Hero abilitiesQ W E, then click a target
WorkersG gather, B build, V advanced build, R return cargo
BuildingsQ W E R train, A S research, T rally, H revive hero, click a queued unit to cancel it
CameraArrow keys, screen edges, middle-drag, wheel to zoom, click the minimap
GroupsCtrl+1–9 to set, 1–9 to select, press twice to jump there
ShortcutsF1 hero, Backspace hall, Space last alert, P pause, Esc cancel
AetherBuild your extractor on the geyser next to your base. Siege engines, flyers and spellcasters need it.
CreepsNeutral camps guard the expansions and pay gold and hero experience. The Aether Wyrm guards the Great Geyser in the middle.
